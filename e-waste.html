♻️ E-Waste Tips
  • 🔐Always factory reset your devices and remove personal data before recycling.
  • 💼Check if manufacturers offer take-back programs (Apple, Samsung, HP all do).
  • 🎁Working devices can be donated to schools or NGOs instead of recycling.
  • ⚠️Never dispose of batteries, CRTs, or ACs in regular trash — they contain toxins.
  • 📦Batteries must be recycled separately — look for dedicated battery collection bins.
